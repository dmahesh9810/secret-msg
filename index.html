How LSB Steganography Operates

        

        
      


      

        

          Least Significant Bit (LSB) Steganography conceals secret data by substituting the lowest-order bit of standard image pixel values. Because changing the 1's bit changes the color by at most 1 level out of 256, the visual alteration is imperceptible to human vision.

• 1 Pixel yields 3 available bits (R, G, B).

            
• 32-Bit Length Header: The first 32 RGB bits encode payload length. Decoder reads this instantly.

            
• Lossless PNG: Lossy JPEG alters pixel values. PNG retains 100% of raw pixel data.
